What this needs from the app

Two new facts to record

When you stopped and how the day ended: you called it a day, the lock ended it, or you took extra time. And whether you did the one thing, asked once in the evening. Curfew records neither today.

What was removed

“Lockout recorded”, the streak count and the nights-in-a-row chip. They measured the app doing its job. Every mark here is something you did.

Patterns have to be earned

An observation appears only with enough days behind it and a clear gap, and it says how many days it is based on. Otherwise the card says there is nothing to report yet.

Readable without colour

Blue and ember pass colour-blindness checks in both light and dark. Early is always up, over is always down, and the table below the charts carries every value.
