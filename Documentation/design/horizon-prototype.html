Curfew visual refresh, third pass

The sky, cut in steps.

A sky drawn one way: hard-edged bands of light that wrap the sun and flatten toward the horizon. The sun is your working day and sets at curfew. On screen is the time you have, the one thing you said today was for, and two buttons. Drag the slider, hold the first button, tick the task.

The bands drift outward while you are working and stop when the Mac locks. Night falls from the top, one band at a time. The moon is in tonight's actual phase.

What changed and why

Nothing you don't act on

The status pill, first warning, extensions left and “back” are gone. Warnings arrive when they arrive. How many extensions remain shows only while you are asking for one. The unlock time shows only once you are locked.

One hand for the sky

No water, bloom, grain or ticks. Every pixel of sky is one of fourteen flat steps. The shapes come from how light falls off around the sun, so they change all day without anyone drawing them.

Glass only on buttons

Two buttons float in the densest part of the sky, where the glass has edges to bend. Nothing else is glass: no chips, no cards, no labels.

One type family

San Francisco throughout, rounded for numerals, headlines and buttons. No serif line, no engraved capitals.

Sky for time, ground for you

Above the horizon: the countdown and what Curfew says. Below it: your task, your note from last night, your answer in the morning.

Icons carry the meaning

Sunset means curfew, sunrise means morning, the lock means locked. Each control and status line leads with one, drawn at the system weight so they sit with SF Symbols.

Rules

Colour is the time of day

Afternoon, the last hour, night. Ember is the only accent and marks the one primary action.

Motion means the day is running

Bands drift while you work and hold still when locked. Numerals roll when they change. Holding a button fills it. Reduce Motion stops the drift and swaps the sunset for a short fade.

Pages follow the system

Schedule, Journal and Settings are plain: warm paper in light mode, warm ink in dark. The sky is kept for Today, the lockout, Sunrise and setup.

Three corners, one grid

Controls 8, panels 20, buttons fully round. Spacing 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 40, 64.
