Screen by screen

1

Welcome

The sky plays one day in about ten seconds with three captions, then rests on one sentence saying what Curfew is. The button is there from the start, so nobody has to wait.

2

One question

Only the curfew time. Weekdays and a 7:00 AM unlock are stated as defaults, and "Change" opens them on their own screen. "Use this schedule" and "No schedule yet" are the two explicit choices.

3

The one permission

Accessibility, alone, with one line saying why. The drag panel sits beside System Settings. Setup comes forward and moves on by itself once access is granted.

4

Two optional asks

Notifications and the Curfew helper as two rows. Continue without touching either is the skip, so there are no Skip buttons.

5

You're set

The sky shows the real time of day and one sentence says when this Mac next locks. The preview is a quiet link. Curfew Plus follows as it does now, then the window is Today.

What stays true

Editing arms nothing

The schedule is recorded on an explicit press and enforced only after real Accessibility trust. A schedule that would lock at once asks first. Setup reopens where it was left.

Keys and VoiceOver

Each time is a slider read as a time. Arrow keys move five minutes, Shift an hour, and the round buttons fifteen. The icon drag has the switch in the list as its other path.

Reduce Motion

The welcome skips the day and shows its resting sentence. Screens cross-fade and the sky holds still. Try the toggle above.
