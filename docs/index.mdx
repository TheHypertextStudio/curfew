Curfew is a macOS app for ending the workday on purpose. Set a schedule, get a
warning as your time runs out, and let Curfew put a full-screen lockout in the
way when the day is over.

## Get started

Set your work hours in Curfew, then choose what happens at the end of the day.
You can use a different schedule for each day, mark days off, and choose whether
Curfew should shut your Mac down after the lockout.

MCP client may send information to its own service, so review that client’s
privacy policy before sharing reflections.
