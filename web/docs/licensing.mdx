## Activate after checkout

When Plus is available, the purchase confirmation page offers
**Activate in Curfew**. Open the installed app when your browser asks, then check
**Settings → About → Curfew Plus** for your plan and purchase email.

Stripe emails your payment receipt. Save it for purchase support.

## Restore a purchase

1. Open **Settings → About → Curfew Plus → Restore Purchase…**.
2. Enter the email address on your receipt and choose **Send Restore Link**.
3. Open the link in your email, then choose **Restore in Curfew**.
4. Check your plan and purchase email in the app.

You can restore a purchase without creating a Curfew account. Restore links
expire; request a new one if yours has expired.

## Enter a key manually

If you already have a license key, or choose the manual option on the purchase
page, save the complete key somewhere private, such as a password manager.

1. Open Curfew and choose **Settings** from the app menu, or press **⌘,**.
2. Open **Settings → About** and find **Curfew Plus**.
3. Choose **Enter a license key** and paste the complete key into **License key**.
4. Click **Activate** and check your plan and purchase email in the panel.

## Use Plus on another Mac

Your license is personal to you and can be used on Macs you own or control,
under the [purchase terms](https://thecurfew.app/terms.html).
Install Curfew and restore your purchase or enter your key on each Mac.
Keep your key and restore links private.

## Offline use and subscriptions

Activation links and purchase restoration require an internet connection.
Entering an existing key and verifying a saved license work offline.
A lifetime license has no access deadline.

Subscriptions refresh on launch and periodically while Curfew is running.
Your saved license works until its access deadline. Reconnect and open Curfew
before that deadline to keep access up to date.

When access expires, Curfew's free schedule and lockout features remain
available.

## Cancel, deactivate, or request a refund

To cancel a subscription or ask about billing, email
[support@thecurfew.app](mailto:support@thecurfew.app) from your purchase email
address and include your order details. Cancellation stops future renewals. See the
[refund policy](https://thecurfew.app/refund.html) for refund requests.

To remove the key from this Mac, open **Settings → About → Curfew Plus** and
click **Deactivate License**. Plus features become unavailable on this Mac;
subscription billing continues until you cancel. Save your key to activate it again later.

## Activation or delivery problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The confirmation page is still preparing your purchase | Wait a minute and try again on the original confirmation page. If it still fails, contact support with your receipt. |
| You closed the page or lost your key | Use **Restore Purchase…** with the email address on your receipt. |
| An activation or restore link has expired | Request a fresh link through **Restore Purchase…**. |
| “The license key format is invalid.” | Copy the entire key again. Paste only the key, without surrounding quotes or other text. |
| “This license key could not be verified.” | Check that you copied the correct key and are using the current official Curfew release. If it still fails, contact support. |
| “This license key is for a different product.” | Make sure you are using your Curfew Plus key. Contact support if that is the key you received. |
| Your subscription has lapsed | Connect to the internet and reopen Curfew. If you recently renewed and access does not return, contact support. |

If the app says activation is disabled for this build, install the official
release. If the message persists, contact support with the app version.
