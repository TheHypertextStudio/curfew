---
title: Install Curfew
description: Download Curfew and choose your first schedule.
---

Curfew requires **macOS 26 or later**. The local app is free and open source.
A public Mac installer has not been released yet. When one is available:

1. Open the [latest GitHub release](https://github.com/TheHypertextStudio/curfew/releases/latest).
2. Download the Mac installer and follow that release's installation instructions.
3. Open Curfew and complete setup. If Curfew asks for Accessibility access, open
   System Settings and enable access for Curfew under Privacy & Security →
   Accessibility. Return to Curfew to continue.
4. Choose your end and resume times in [your weekly schedule](/schedules).

Read the release notes before upgrading. They identify version-specific changes
and requirements. Until a release is published, the
[release page](https://github.com/TheHypertextStudio/curfew/releases) will not
have an installer to download.

## Before your first lockout

Save your work when Curfew warns you. Review your [exception budgets](/exceptions)
and leave automatic shutdown off until you understand its behavior.

An account is optional. You can set and enforce a local schedule without signing
in. See [account setup](/accounts) if you want device or assistant connections.

## Something went wrong?

Check that your Mac meets the release requirements and that Curfew has the access
requested during setup. [Contact support](https://curfew.hypertext.studio/support)
with your Curfew version, macOS version, and what happened.
