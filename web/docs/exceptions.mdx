---
title: Extensions and overrides
description: Use limited extra time deliberately.
---

## Before lockout

An extension adds time before the end of your work window when the warning stage
and your remaining extension budget allow it. Review the extension duration and
weekly budget in Curfew's settings.

## During lockout

An override lets you return to urgent work when you have budget remaining.
Use the override action on the lockout screen, enter a reason, and complete its
confirmation. Curfew adds deliberate friction so using an exception is a choice.

Extension and override budgets are separate. Their remaining counts appear in
Curfew; do not assume that one available budget means the other is available.

If you regularly need an exception at the same time, review your schedule.
