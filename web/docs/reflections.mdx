title: Reflections and weekly review
description: Capture intentions, reflect at the end of the day, and review or export what you wrote.
---

Curfew offers a morning reflection at the day's first session and an evening
reflection on the lockout screen. Record what you intended to do and how the
day went, or choose **Skip**.

## Choose your prompts

1. Open **Journal** in the main window and select **Prompts**.
2. Enable the morning and evening reflections you want.
3. Add or edit questions under **Morning prompts** and **Evening prompts**.
4. Choose a text, rating, or mood answer and check its preview.

You can reorder or remove questions and restore defaults. Saved answers keep
their original questions.

## Read and export entries

Open **Journal → Entries** to revisit answers from the current calendar week.
Use the export menu to save that week as **Markdown** or **JSON**, or choose
**Copy this week**. Markdown is useful for reading; JSON is useful for processing
with another tool.

To save older entries, use the [command-line export](/cli#export-older-reflections).
Choose a date range that includes everything you want to keep, and check the
saved file before uninstalling.

Review **This Week** for your activity and exception use.

## Privacy

Reflections are stored on this Mac. [Connected assistants](/mcp-guide) can read them
and send them to their providers. Review [privacy and permissions](/privacy)
before connecting.
