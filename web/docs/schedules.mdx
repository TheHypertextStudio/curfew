---
title: Schedules and warnings
description: Set end and resume times for each day and understand what happens at curfew.
---

## Set your week

Open Curfew's schedule settings. Set an end time and a resume time for each day.
Days can have different hours, and you can turn a day off. Check both times: a
resume time is when Curfew allows work again after that day's boundary.

## Warnings

Curfew escalates its reminders as the end time approaches. You can adjust the
warning intervals in settings. Early warnings give you time to save your work;
later warnings make the approaching boundary harder to miss.

## Lockout

At your end time, Curfew covers every display. Lockout remains until your
scheduled resume time unless you use an available [override](/exceptions).
Curfew can retain the active lockout across relaunches; quitting and reopening
it is not the normal way to request more time.

Curfew is a commitment device. A determined person who controls the Mac can
bypass it. It is not a replacement for macOS security controls.
