---
title: Warnings and lockout
description: Know what happens as your stopping time approaches, during lockout, and when work resumes.
---

Check **Today** or Curfew's menu bar item for your status and time remaining.

## Before work ends

With the default intervals, warnings begin 30 minutes before lockout and
increase in urgency as time runs out. The 30- and 15-minute warnings are
notifications; on-screen warnings begin at 5 minutes. Save documents and
finish calls before lockout.

Change intervals in **Settings → Warnings → Before lockout**.

## During lockout

Curfew covers your displays and blocks dismissal shortcuts. The lockout screen
shows when access resumes. Lockout persists across app restarts.

For temporary access, use [an emergency override](/exceptions). Lockout ends at
the scheduled resume time unless another active limit applies.

## Optional shutdown

In **Settings → Warnings → After lockout**, turn on **Shut down this Mac** and
choose a delay after lockout begins.

<Warning>
Auto shutdown turns off the Mac and can interrupt unsaved work. Save your work
before the boundary.
</Warning>

If shutdown controls are unavailable, follow the explanation in the panel.

## If enforcement is degraded

Check **Settings → Protection**. Missing Accessibility
access or a helper problem can weaken enforcement. Use the repair action and
[troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting).
