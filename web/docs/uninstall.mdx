---
title: Update or uninstall
description: Keep Curfew up to date or remove the app and its local information deliberately.
---

## Update Curfew

Save your work, then choose **Check for Updates…** from Curfew's app menu and
follow the prompt. Check the installed version in **Settings → About** afterward.
If updating fails, use the download from the
[official website](https://thecurfew.app/).

## Before uninstalling

Export [reflection entries](/reflections) you want to keep, save your Plus key,
and note any schedule settings you want to reuse.

The Journal export covers the current week only. For older entries, use the
[command-line export](/cli#export-older-reflections) with a long enough date
range. Check the saved file before continuing.

<Warning>
Uninstall permanently removes local settings, the saved license key, activity,
reflections, and background state. Subscription billing continues until you cancel.
</Warning>

For cancellation, follow [billing help](/licensing).

## Remove Curfew

1. Open **Settings → Advanced → Uninstall Curfew…**.
2. Review the confirmation and choose **Uninstall** when ready.
3. Follow helper-removal prompts. Resolve any displayed error before retrying.
4. Review the result. Choose **Reveal in Finder**, then drag **Curfew.app** from
   Applications to the Trash to finish.

If the result lists errors, send the error text and app version to
[hello@hypertext.studio](mailto:hello@hypertext.studio).
