---
title: Accounts and connections
description: Sign in, recover access, and manage connected devices and assistants.
---

You can set and enforce a schedule on your Mac without an account. Sign in when
you want to manage connected devices or assistant permissions.

## Sign in

Open [your Curfew account](https://thecurfew.app/account). Use the
passkey saved on your device or in your password manager, or a connected Apple
or Google account when that option is offered.

## Create an account

Enter your email address, then create a passkey. During security setup, save
your recovery codes somewhere you can reach if you lose access to that passkey.
Curfew shows each set once.

## Manage sign-in methods

Open **Account & security** in your Curfew account. Curfew may ask you to
confirm your identity before changing a sign-in method.

| Action | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Add a passkey | Under **Passkeys**, choose **Add passkey** and finish your browser or password manager's prompt. |
| Name a passkey | Choose **Rename** beside it, enter a name you recognize, then choose **Save name**. |
| Connect Apple or Google | Choose **Link Apple** or **Link Google** when offered, then follow the sign-in and security setup. |
| Remove a sign-in method | Choose **Remove** beside it and confirm which method to remove. Add another method first if it is your only way to sign in. |

Removing a method from Curfew does not delete the passkey saved in your password
manager or delete your Apple or Google account.

## Recover access

Start from **Recover your account** on the sign-in page. Enter your email
address, then verify that you own it. Curfew will show the recovery methods
available for that account. If you previously saved recovery codes, choose
that method and enter an unused code when prompted. You can then register a
replacement passkey and save new recovery codes.

Never send a recovery code to support or an assistant.

## Replace recovery codes

In **Account & security → Recovery codes**, choose **Replace recovery codes**.
Save the new set, then choose **I saved these recovery codes**. Each code can
be used once.

Earlier codes stop working. Replacing the set also removes earlier permissions
to control Curfew remotely; reconnect devices and assistants when prompted.

## Connect an assistant

Review the requested permissions before approving a connection. Each Mac must
opt in before an assistant can request a lock. You can disconnect assistants in
your account.

An offline Mac must reconnect and apply a queued lock request before it locks.

Open **Integrations → Connected assistants** to review or **Disconnect** a
connection. For a locally configured assistant, also follow the
[assistant removal instructions](/mcp-guide); its local configuration is separate.

Open **Devices** to review your connected Macs. Add a Mac from Curfew's
**Settings → Account** on that Mac.

## Download account data

In **Account & security → Account data**, choose **Download account data**.
Save the downloaded JSON file somewhere private. It includes hosted account
records and encrypted sync data; it does not decrypt your settings or copy
local-only Mac data.

## Delete an account

Deleting an account permanently removes its hosted account and synced settings,
signs out its sessions, and disconnects its devices and assistants. Your Macs
keep their local data.

**Deleting an account does not cancel subscription billing.** For cancellation
or purchase help, contact [support@thecurfew.app](mailto:support@thecurfew.app)
from your purchase email before deleting the account. See
[license recovery and billing help](/licensing) and the
[refund policy](https://thecurfew.app/refund).

1. Open **Account & security → Delete account**.
2. Choose **Delete account…** and read the confirmation.
3. Type **DELETE**, then choose **Permanently delete account**.

You return to sign-in after deletion succeeds. If Curfew cannot confirm the
result, choose **Check account status** before trying again.
