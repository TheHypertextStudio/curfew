Curfew keeps your schedule, preferences, activity, and reflection answers on
your Mac. Core features work without an account or purchase.

## Permissions you may see

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessibility | Blocks dismissal shortcuts during lockout | Required to complete setup |
| Notifications | Sends warnings before lockout | Optional |
| Calendar | Reads today's events for display | Optional Plus feature at launch |
| Background items and helper approval | Starts and maintains the enforcement helper | Needed for the corresponding enforcement components |

macOS may call Accessibility **Device Control and Data Access**. The system
explains that this permission can allow broad control of apps and access to
data. Curfew uses it to block dismissal shortcuts during lockout. Turning it
on may require Touch ID or an administrator password.

**Background recovery** restarts Curfew if it stops. **Background protection**
uses a helper to maintain enforcement. macOS may ask an administrator to allow
background activity for all users. Check both states in **Curfew → Settings →
Protection** (**General** in earlier versions). See [setup](/install) for the
approval steps.

## When information leaves this Mac

- **A connected assistant:** can read status, schedules, and saved reflections.
  Its provider may process that information. To [disconnect](/mcp-guide), remove
  Curfew from the assistant's configuration and fully quit and restart it.
  Locally configured assistants can still read saved information while the
  app toggle is off.
- **iCloud sync at the Plus launch:** uses your Apple Account to exchange
  schedule and device information. Written reflection answers stay on this Mac.
- **Plus activation and restoration:** activation links redeem a token with
  the license service. Restoration sends your purchase email to the license
  service to deliver a restore link. Subscription licensing periodically
  contacts that service to refresh access. Saved lifetime license checks work
  offline.
- **Updates:** connects to the update feed to check for and download releases.
- **Exports or support requests:** contain what you choose to save or send.
  Review files and screenshots before sharing.

Buying Plus provides purchase information to Stripe and the license service.

## Export or remove information

Use [Journal export](/reflections) to save entries as Markdown or JSON.
Use the [uninstall flow](/uninstall) to remove local state after saving what
you want to keep. [Cancel subscription billing](/licensing) separately. Manage
copies held by iCloud or an assistant through that provider's settings.
