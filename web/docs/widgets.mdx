## Add Curfew to your desktop

1. Install Curfew in Applications, open it, and [activate Plus](/licensing).
2. Control-click the desktop and choose **Edit Widgets**, or open Notification
   Center and choose **Edit Widgets**.
3. Search for **Curfew**.
4. Choose a small, medium, or large widget and add it to your desktop or
   Notification Center.

The widget shows Curfew's status and countdown. Larger sizes also show schedule
and activity information.

## If the widget is missing or stale

Open Curfew once after installing or updating it, confirm Plus is active, and
look in the widget gallery again. If a widget is already present, compare it
with **Today** in the app.

macOS controls refresh timing, so a widget may lag behind the app. Use the
app's status when a boundary or exception has just changed.
