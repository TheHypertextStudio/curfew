Check your app version in **Settings → About** and status in **Today**.

## Setup will not continue

The schedule step requires opening **Open Schedule** from the walkthrough.
The permissions step requires Accessibility access. Open **System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, enable Curfew, and return to Getting
Started. Notifications are optional.

Reopen setup through **Settings → General → Review Setup…** if needed.
Read the final schedule summary before enabling.

## Lockout or keyboard blocking is not working

Open **Settings → General → Protection**. Use the repair action
for missing Accessibility access. If the helper is unavailable, check
**Settings → General → Background protection** and its error.

If enforcement remains degraded after updating, send support the status text
and app version.

## My schedule edit did not apply

Check **Schedule → Pending Change**. More permissive edits wait 24 hours;
stricter edits apply at the next day boundary. More permissive edits also
wait until an active lockout ends. See [schedule rules](/schedules).

## I need access during lockout

Use **Need to get back in?** on the lockout screen. You need an available
override, a reason, a cooldown, and a hold to confirm. See
[extensions and emergency overrides](/exceptions).

## The assistant cannot find Curfew or a request stays pending

Check **Allow assistants on this Mac** in **Settings → Connections**, fully quit
and reopen the assistant, and confirm its configuration points to Curfew's
installed path. Keep Curfew open to review requests. **Read only**
blocks changes. See [assistant setup](/mcp-guide).

## Plus will not activate or a feature is missing

Follow [activation troubleshooting](/licensing). Sync and Calendar are not
available yet.

## Ask for help

Email [hello@hypertext.studio](mailto:hello@hypertext.studio) with:

- Your macOS version and Curfew version from **Settings → About**.
- What you tried, what happened, and the exact error.
- Whether it started after an update or permission change.
- For purchases, your checkout email and receipt or order details.

Remove keys, private reflections, calendar details, and other personal
information from screenshots.
