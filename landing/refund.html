Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with Curfew Plus. If it isn't the right fit, we offer a straightforward refund.

14-day refunds

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your Curfew Plus purchase, for any reason. Just email hello@hypertext.studio from the address you used at checkout, or include your order number from the Stripe receipt. We'll process it promptly.

How refunds are issued

Curfew Plus is sold through Stripe as the merchant of record, so refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe. It can take a few business days for the funds to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Subscriptions

A Curfew Plus subscription renews automatically. You can cancel anytime to stop future renewals; Plus stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then turns off on its own. The 14-day window above applies to your most recent charge.

About your license key

One-time Curfew Plus keys are perpetual and verified offline. Because they are not remotely revocable, a refunded one-time key may technically continue to unlock Plus on your machines; requesting a refund is your agreement to stop using Curfew Plus and delete the key, and we trust you to honor that. Subscription keys carry an expiry and are refreshed online while active, so they stop unlocking Plus on their own once a subscription ends.

After 14 days

Beyond the 14-day window we generally can't offer refunds, but if something has gone wrong, reach out anyway — hello@hypertext.studio — and we'll do our best to make it right.
