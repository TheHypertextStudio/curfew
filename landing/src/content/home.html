Frequently asked questions

Does Curfew work on Windows?

Curfew is available for Mac only, on macOS 26 or later. There isn’t a Windows or Linux version yet. Cross-platform functionality is coming soon with Curfew Plus.

Is Curfew free?

Yes. Curfew is free and open source. Curfew Plus is not available for purchase yet.

Do I need an account?

No. You can set schedules and use lockout without an account.

What if I need more time?

During lockout, you can request an override. Enter a reason and confirm to use one of your remaining overrides.

Can I bypass it?

Yes. Curfew is a commitment device, and a determined person can bypass it. It adds a deliberate step between your finish time and more work.

How do I install it?

The public Mac download is coming soon. Check the download page for availability and installation instructions. Curfew requires macOS 26 or later.
