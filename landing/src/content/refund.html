Published October 14, 2026. Curfew Plus purchases from Hypertext Studio include a straightforward 14-day refund promise.

14-day refunds

You can request a full refund for any reason within 14 days of purchase or your most recent renewal charge. Email support@thecurfew.app from the address used at checkout, or include the order number on your Stripe receipt. Do not send full card details. We will process the request promptly.

How refunds are issued

Stripe processes Curfew payments and returns refunds to the original payment method. It can take a few business days for the funds to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the interval shown at checkout. Cancel through billing controls or contact support to stop future renewals. Unless refunded, Plus remains available until the end of the paid period. The 14-day refund window includes the most recent renewal charge, not just the initial purchase. Canceling alone does not request a refund; ask support for one. Deleting a Curfew account does not itself cancel billing.

Offline license honor system

One-time keys are perpetual and verified offline. They are not remotely revocable, so a refunded key may technically continue to unlock Plus. Requesting a refund is your agreement to stop using the refunded Plus license and delete the key. We trust you to honor that. Subscription keys expire and refresh online while active; they stop unlocking Plus when their validity ends.

After 14 days and statutory rights

Beyond 14 days we generally cannot offer a discretionary refund, but contact support if something has gone wrong. Mandatory consumer, cancellation, and refund rights under applicable law remain available. This clarification does not reduce any existing purchase or accrued refund right.
