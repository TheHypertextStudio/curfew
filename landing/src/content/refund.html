Policy changes and previous versions.

Request a refund within 14 days

You can request a full refund for any reason within 14 days of purchase or your most recent renewal charge.

Email support@thecurfew.app from the address used at checkout, or include the order number on your Stripe receipt. Do not send full card details. We will process your request promptly.

Receiving your refund

Stripe returns the refund to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may take a few business days to show it.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Subscriptions renew at the interval shown at checkout. Use billing controls or contact support to stop future renewals. Unless refunded, Plus stays available until the end of the paid period.

Canceling does not request a refund; ask support for one. Deleting your Curfew account does not cancel billing.

After a license refund

Lifetime keys do not expire and are not remotely revocable, so a refunded key may continue to unlock Plus. By requesting a refund, you agree to stop using the refunded Plus license and delete the key. We trust you to honor that agreement.

Subscription keys stop unlocking Plus when their validity ends.

After 14 days and your legal rights

We generally cannot offer a discretionary refund after 14 days, but contact support if something has gone wrong. Mandatory consumer, cancellation, and refund rights remain available.

This update does not reduce any existing purchase or refund right. See the Terms for license conditions.
