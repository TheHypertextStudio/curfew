Data Retention Policy

How long Curfew keeps your information and how to delete it.

Effective October 14, 2026.

Policy changes and previous versions.

On your devices

Your schedules, preferences, license keys, wake settings, and integration settings stay until you remove them.

Activity and reflections keep a rolling 52 weeks of history. Cleanup runs at day rollover while Curfew is open. If the app is stopped or cleanup fails, older records can remain until cleanup succeeds.

Local assistant change requests and their approval or denial records have no fixed automatic deletion period. They remain until removed.

Browser review answers are kept temporarily while a decision is pending and cleared when it finishes. Browser decision history follows a 90-day rotation policy. The current log remains until it rotates. This history contains the hostname, decision, and type of access allowed, rather than full URLs or answers.

Camera frames are never saved. Presence results are replaced by the next reading or cleared when capture stops. Manage private iCloud records through Apple’s service.

Hosted accounts

  • Encrypted content stays while needed for sync, until you delete it or your account.
  • Sign-in sessions, assistant permissions, and related security records become eligible for cleanup 30 days after they expire or are revoked. Some short-lived verification records can be removed as soon as they expire.
  • Remote-control requests, results, and confirmations become eligible for cleanup 30 days after the request.
  • Remote-unlock and account security history has a retention cutoff of 365 days.
  • Records used to protect against abusive requests become eligible for cleanup 30 days after last use. Our account service does not save raw IP addresses in authentication records. Hosting providers may keep connection logs separately.
  • Purchase, payment, and refund records stay as needed for licensing, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Account deletion removes their direct link to your account. It does not delete payment-provider records.

Removal happens during scheduled cleanup, so some information can remain after its cutoff.

Exporting and deleting your account

Export your account information before you delete your Curfew account. The export includes purchases, devices, assistant permissions, security history, and encrypted content. An enrolled device or your Recovery Key is still needed to read the encrypted content.

Successful deletion removes your account and its encrypted content, devices, remote-control information, and recovery information during the deletion request. It also closes connections and revokes access. Purchase records remain separately, and retained security history is no longer linked directly to your account.

If deletion fails, Curfew reports an error without confirming completion. Contact support if you cannot complete it.

Account deletion does not erase local files, operating-system backups, private iCloud records, information already shared with assistants, Docket or Athena, or payment-provider records. Manage those copies through the relevant service. Removing the app may leave settings and local data. Export anything you want to keep and contact support if you need help removing local copies.

Cancel subscriptions before deleting your account. Offline licenses remain governed by their purchase and refund terms.

Temporary browser reviews

Review data and cleanup timing

The Chrome extension keeps your initial justification only while a challenge is pending, so a page reload does not lose it. It does not keep completed justifications or challenge answers.

Curfew temporarily saves review inputs on your Mac, then clears your justification and answer when the review finishes. Unfinished requests become stale after 60 seconds. Completed review records become eligible for removal after 120 seconds. Cleanup runs while the integration is active; stopped software or cleanup failures can leave records longer.
