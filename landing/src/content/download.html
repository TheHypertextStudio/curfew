Download Curfew

A curfew for your Mac. Free and open source, for macOS 26 or later.

The public Mac download is coming soon. The installer will be available here when it is ready.

View the source on GitHub.

When the download is available

  1. Download the disk image and drag Curfew into Applications.
  2. Open Curfew, set your weekly hours, and review all seven days.
  3. Choose your warnings and exception limits, then enable your schedule when ready.

Read the installation guide or get help.
