Download Curfew

A curfew for your Mac. Free and open source, for macOS 26 or later.

Download for Mac

Get started

  1. Download the latest Mac release from GitHub.
  2. Open Curfew and choose when your workday ends and resumes.
  3. Set your warnings and any exceptions you want to allow.

Read the installation guide or get help.
