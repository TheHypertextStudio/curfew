Curfew 0.0.17

Keep background recovery on track after a clock correction.

Clock corrections

Curfew no longer waits for the old clock time to return before restarting an interrupted app. It keeps only the remaining retry delay, while preserving its protection against repeated failed launches and duplicate starts.

Updating

Download Curfew and replace your existing copy in Applications. Keep your app data so your schedule, history, and saved preferences remain available. Curfew requires macOS 26 or later.

Your local schedule works without an account. Curfew Plus remains unavailable for purchase. Existing license holders can use the license guide for activation and purchase restoration.
