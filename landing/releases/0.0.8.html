Curfew 0.0.8

Curfew’s accounts and connections now live at thecurfew.app.

What’s changed

Account pages now have a clearer layout for sign-in methods, recovery, and connected Macs. Account connections use Curfew’s own domain.

Updating

Download Curfew and move it to Applications, replacing your existing copy. Curfew requires macOS 26 or later.

If you previously connected an account or an assistant, sign in again to reconnect it. You can continue using your local schedule without an account.
