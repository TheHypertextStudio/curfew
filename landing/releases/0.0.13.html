Curfew 0.0.13

Choose when your working day ends, and let Curfew help you keep that boundary.

A schedule you choose

Review all seven days before accepting a weekly schedule. Setup explains the Mac permissions Curfew needs, and you can leave automatic lockouts off until you are ready. You can return to setup from Settings to review your choices.

Clearer settings and permission recovery

Settings groups protection, lockouts, presence, shutdown, and protected work into clearer sections. Setup stays within reach from Protection. Permission warnings reflect the Mac's current access, and repair actions take you to System Settings when access is missing.

Time to stop, and a way back

Warnings give you time to wrap up before your scheduled lockout. The rest screen shows when you can return, with the extension and recovery options allowed by your settings. Background protection keeps the schedule available when the main window is closed.

Accounts and connections

Curfew accounts now live at thecurfew.app. Manage your passkeys, connected Apple or Google sign-in, recovery codes, Macs, and assistant connections from your account. You can also download your account data or delete your account. The account guide explains what each action changes.

If you connected an account or an assistant before this update, sign in again to reconnect it. Your local schedule works without an account.

Accurate subscription status

Curfew updates Plus access when a subscription expires and removes the old activation confirmation. Purchase restoration stays available, and an expiry does not interrupt a restore request. Your local schedule continues to work without Plus.

Apple silicon and Intel Macs

Background protection, local assistant connections, and browser protection are included for both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Updating

Download Curfew and move it to Applications, replacing your existing copy. Curfew requires macOS 26 or later. Keep your existing app data when updating so your schedule, history, and saved preferences remain available.

Curfew Plus

Curfew Plus is not available for purchase yet. If you already have a license, the license guide explains activation and purchase restoration.
